👣 STORIES LEADING TO THE DARK TOWER

'Salem's Lot (1975)

Features Father Callahan who plays a major role in The Dark Tower.

Vampiric characters are present in The Dark Tower (as in Salem's Lot, of course)

The Stand (1978)

Features Randall Flagg, who is a major antagonist in the Dark Tower series.

The Talisman (1984) - Co-written with Peter Straub, this novel

Introduces the concept of parallel worlds and shares thematic elements with the Dark Tower series."Black House" (2001), further expands on these connections.

Eyes of the Dragon (1984)

It (1986)

Features the character Pennywise the Dancing Clown, who is also a part of the Dark Tower universe.

Insomnia (1994)

Explores the concept of the "beams" and their importance in the Dark Tower world.

Characters play a significant role in the later books of the Dark Tower series.

Hearts in Atlantis (1999)

Features characters connected to the Dark Tower series and explores themes related to the conflict between good and evil.

The Little Sisters of Eluria (1998)

A prequel to the events of "The Gunslinger," providing additional backstory to Roland's early adventures.

Originally published in Everything's Eventual

The Plant (2000)

Note, this story is incomplete and likely never to be finished.

Everything's Eventual (2002)

A short story by the same name as the collection in which it's published.

Charlie the Choo-Choo (2016)

A children's book written under the pseudonym Beryl Evans that appears in the Dark Tower series.
